Rivoire - Where Elegant Tradition Meets Piazza della Signoria


One of Florence's most iconic cafés, since 1872.


Overlooking Piazza della Signoria, Rivoire is one of Florence's most elegant places for breakfast, lunch, aperitivo and snacks. Founded by Enrico Rivoire, chocolatier to the Royal House of Savoy.

Rivoire is also closely linked to Florentine cocktail history. It was one of the favorite cafés of Count Camillo Negroni, who is credited with inventing the world-famous Negroni cocktail just a few steps away in Piazza della Signoria.


Famous for its artisanal hot chocolate, homemade pastries and refined Italian cuisine, Rivoire combines history with one of the city's most spectacular terraces overlooking Palazzo Vecchio.
