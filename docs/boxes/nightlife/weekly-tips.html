Weekly Tips

TIPS
FOR THE WEEKEND 03-04th OCTOBER IN FLORENCE




EVENTS and MUSEUMS:

Sunday at the museum: on 3 October the state museums of Florence are open to free admission; it will be possible to visit the Uffizi, Palazzo Pitti, Boboli, Bargello, the Medici Chapels, the Accademia Gallery, the Archaeological Museum and Palazzo Davanzati for free

Antiques Biennale: Palazzo Corsini is hosting the BIAF – Florence International Biennale of Antiques – until October 4, bringing together around 80 galleries showcasing some of the very best of the Italian and international art market

Broken. The Power of the Fragment: Palazzo Strozzi is hosting a major exhibition exploring the role of fragments in the history of sculpture, from archaeology to contemporary art. The show brings together fascinating works ranging from ancient Egypt to Michelangelo, from classical Greece to Rodin, as well as contemporary artists such as Louise Bourgeois, Francesco Vezzoli and Danh Vo

MARKETS:

Plant & Flower Market: from Friday to Sunday, the Giardino dell'Orticultura will host flower growers from all over Tuscany, with a great selection of unusual plants and flowers in its autumn edition. Three days entirely dedicated to plants, flowers and gardening (H 9/19)

International Ceramics Fair: in Piazza Santa Croce, you'll find the work of over 90 ceramic artists from all over Europe. From everyday objects to sculptures, jewellery and design pieces, made using maiolica, stoneware, porcelain, raku and many other techniques (H 10/19)

Intrecci di Artigianato: in Piazza Ognissanti, you'll be surrounded by the aromas of local products from small farms and the creativity of local artisans, with jewellery, fashion accessories, honey, cheese, knitwear, cosmetics, ceramics and much more (H 8/20)

Creative Mani Factory: over the weekend, Manifattura Tabacchi will host a cool artisan market, perfect if you're into independent design, handmade products and limited-edition creations (H 10/20)

Artefacemdo: on Saturday, Piazza dei Ciompi will once again be filled with artisans and creatives selling jewellery and accessories, leather goods, clothes and knitwear, ceramics, illustrations, home decor and much more (H 10/20)

Savonarola Antiques Market: on Sunday, Piazza Savonarola will host a market dedicated to antiques, vintage items, crafts and collectibles. Have a wander around the stalls and see what you can find, from collectible pieces and curiosities to books, paintings, prints, porcelain, furniture and more (H 9/19)

Only Usato: on Saturday at Orti Dipinti (Borgo Pinti 76), there'll be an event all about reuse and second-hand shopping, with clothes, accessories, vinyl records, books, plus workshops, food, music and much more (H 11/20)

The Pumpkin & Straw Kingdom: until November 1st, the autumn festival returns to Parco di Villa Montalvo in Campi Bisenzio, with giant pumpkins, huge straw sculptures, shows, workshops and a whole amusement park (H 10/20)
